About Obselite Athlete
The best in skills development

O.A is a youth-driven sports and wellness training facility supporting vulnerable youth in Nelson Mandela Bay. We collaborate with an NGO to offer weekly sports and development sessions for primary school children in the local community.

Our efforts are limited by the capacity of the aftercare facility, which currently serves only a select group of youngsters chosen by our NGO partner. To reach more youth and have a greater impact, we rely on donations.

Your support, in any amount, is essential to our mission. Every contribution helps us assist more young people in the community and make a meaningful difference in their development and well-being.

All donations are welcomed and deeply appreciated.
